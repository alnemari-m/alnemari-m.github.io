Research Interests

What I Work On

My research sits at the intersection of efficient deep learning and real-world deployment — making powerful AI models small, fast, and reliable enough for the edge.

Edge AI & TinyML

Deploying DNNs on microcontrollers (ARM Cortex-M, RISC-V), Jetson, and FPGAs under strict latency, memory, and power budgets. Building portable inference engines.

Neural Network Compression

Filter pruning, quantization (INT8/binary), tensor decomposition, and knowledge distillation — achieving 10-100x compression with minimal accuracy loss.

In-Memory AI Computing

RRAM crossbar-based analog accelerators that break the von Neumann bottleneck. Designing fault-tolerant neural architectures for resistive memory devices.

Conformal Prediction for ML

Distribution-free statistical methods providing coverage guarantees for compression hyperparameters — replacing expensive grid search with reliable surrogates.

Binary Neural Networks

32x compression through 1-bit weights and activations. Studying loss landscape geometry and training dynamics to make BNNs practical and reliable.

Neural Operators & Geometry

Equivariant neural networks and structured pruning that respects symmetry. Geometric deep learning for scientific applications.
