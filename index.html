// What I Work On

Research Interests

Edge AI & TinyML

Deploying deep learning models on microcontrollers (ARM Cortex-M, RISC-V) and edge GPUs under strict latency, memory, and power budgets.

Neural Network Compression

Filter pruning, quantization, knowledge distillation, and tensor decomposition to reduce DNN computational complexity by 10-100x without sacrificing accuracy.

HW-SW Co-Design

Joint optimization of neural architectures and hardware accelerators — including RRAM-based analog in-memory computing and FPGA-based inference engines.

Conformal Prediction for ML

Distribution-free statistical methods that provide coverage guarantees for compression hyperparameter selection, replacing expensive grid search with reliable surrogates.
